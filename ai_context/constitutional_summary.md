# Constitutional Summary

## Purpose

Read this first in new AI sessions. It is a compact bootstrap summary, not a replacement for the full constitutional documents in `docs/constitutional/`.

The Relocation App is a layered symbolic intelligence platform. It is not a monolithic astrology chatbot, hidden recommendation engine, or mystical certainty machine.

---

# Layer Architecture

## Layer 1 - Truth

Layer 1 owns astronomical and geometric truth:

- planetary positions,
- relocation geometry,
- coordinate validity,
- angularity,
- aspects,
- house positions,
- search conditions,
- sampling,
- and computational reality.

Layer 1 is deterministic, inspectable, objective, and independently verifiable. It must not interpret, optimize, moralize, psychologically frame, or alter truth to satisfy user desire.

## Layer 2 - Symbolic Ontology

Layer 2 owns symbolic definitions:

- archetypes,
- dignity systems,
- orb doctrines,
- aspect frameworks,
- house meanings,
- and ontology plugins.

Layer 2 may interpret truth through a declared symbolic framework, but it may never rewrite geometry. Symbolic systems may disagree; no ontology is permanently privileged as universal truth.

## Layer 3 - Intentional Interpretation

Layer 3 owns contextual interpretation relative to user goals, lived context, priorities, and tradeoffs.

Layer 3 is collaborative, conversational, probabilistic, and user-intent driven. It may clarify and compare, but it must not fabricate certainty, manipulate the user, or alter Layer 1 truth or Layer 2 definitions.

## Layer 4 - Exploratory Optimization

Layer 4 owns optional exploration of what else may be possible:

- adjacent opportunities,
- alternative symbolic strategies,
- optimization pathways,
- and broader relocation possibilities.

Layer 4 is invitation-sensitive and subordinate to Layer 3 intentionality. It may suggest and compare, but must not hijack, pressure, impose symbolic agendas, or turn optimization into destiny theater.

---

# Forbidden Crossings

- Layer 2 may not alter Layer 1 truth.
- Layer 3 may not fabricate or dynamically rewrite Layer 2 symbolism.
- Layer 4 may not override Layer 3 intentionality.
- Runtime, renderer, hydration, cache, and observer systems may not invent symbolism or manipulate interpretation.
- AI systems may not fabricate certainty, create hidden ontologies, or bypass doctrine.
- Exploratory language may not become operational truth.

Truth flows upward. Interpretation never flows downward.

---

# Epistemic Doctrine

Honest uncertainty is superior to symbolic overreach.

The system must tolerate ambiguity, unresolved states, competing interpretations, and limits of evidence. Not every life event maps cleanly to astrology. Absence of explanation is preferable to false explanation.

Avoid:

- fake certainty,
- mystical theater,
- forced symbolic coherence,
- moralized archetypes,
- destiny declarations,
- and psychologically manipulative reassurance.

Prefer:

- clear uncertainty,
- collaborative discovery,
- structurally grounded interpretation,
- tradeoff honesty,
- and professional restraint.

---

# Runtime And Renderer Sovereignty

Runtime systems own scheduling, orchestration, cache behavior, hydration, cancellation, and observer-safe state.

Renderers own presentation and visual display.

Neither runtime nor renderer owns truth, symbolic meaning, user intention, or optimization authority.

Hydration must remain sanitized, inspectable, and bounded. Sandboxes must remain removable, reversible, dev-only unless explicitly promoted, and unable to seize production renderer ownership.

Rendering visualizes truth; it does not compute or alter truth.

---

# Purification Principle

Architectural purity is easier to preserve than to restore.

Purification audits exist to detect:

- layer contamination,
- symbolic language leaking into truth computation,
- runtime inventing semantics,
- optimization overriding intentionality,
- hidden heuristics,
- and exploratory concepts hardening into production truth.

Rollback is preferable to contaminated forward progress.

---

# Professional Trust And AI Behavior

Professional trust is sacred.

AI should behave as a careful assistant, organizer, translator, comparison aide, and collaborator. It must not become a symbolic authority, therapist replacement, spiritual guru, or certainty machine.

AI must:

- admit uncertainty,
- identify risks,
- separate speculation from implementation,
- preserve rollback discipline,
- respect layer sovereignty,
- and keep implementation inspectable.

Architecture itself is part of the product.
