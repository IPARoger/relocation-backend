## 1. Executive Abstract

Chat 10 marks the project’s transition from renderer-heavy work into real product surfaces: comparison, city intelligence, city profiles, settings prototypes, and governance infrastructure. The central architectural realization is that **comparison is the primary decision workspace**; map is discovery, city profile is relocation context, favorites are storage, profiles are identity.

The chat also matured governance from backend/math safety into a full AI-assisted product operating system: constrained reading, plan-first discipline, versioned prototypes, scoped commits, browser validation hierarchy, and cost containment.

## 2. Major Product Pivots

- **Comparison became center of gravity:** Not map, city page, settings, or notes — comparison is where users weigh options and make decisions.
- **City intelligence reframed:** From possible travel guide to relocation dossier answering “Could I realistically live here?” — not tourism, rankings, or vacation planning.
- **Cup-holder doctrine:** City intelligence is supportive convenience, not the engine. Astrology/comparison remain primary.
- **Design → infrastructure shift:** Enough UI structure exists to begin Supabase, persistence, APIs, caching, and onboarding.
- **Rain/Virga paused:** Roadmap moved toward accounts, comparison, city intelligence, settings, backend persistence.
- **Interesting ≠ important:** Anti-sprawl sequencing — defer glyphs, typography, color finalization, mobile, AI onboarding until structure stabilizes.

## 4. Key Doctrines Created Or Reinforced

### Product
- Cities = columns, facts = rows (city axis doctrine).
- Comparison controls: Hide, Reorder, Replace, Add City; Replace/Add create new records; history preserved.
- City snapshot metrics: Cost, Safety, Stability, Expat Community, Infrastructure, Weather, Population (population de-emphasized).
- Use **Stable** not **High**; section: **Stability & Freedom**.
- **For Your Stated Intentions** (not “For Your Intentions”) — protects against oracle framing.
- Citizenship from active profile birthplace; language has three layers (Official / English Spoken / Your Language).
- Single visible currency at a time (Local / USD / Home).
- AI consumes facts; does not generate facts or rankings.

### Visual / Design
- Two related color systems: Map Language (expressive, functional) and Workspace Language (desaturated ~85%, editorial).
- Design-system mindset emerging: badges, pills, dropdowns, accordions, spacing, animation should be standardized across pages.
- Quiet, premium, restrained aesthetic; reject cosmic kitsch, emoji astrology, colorful new-age noise.
- Wheel glow should feel alive, not decorative.

### Governance
- Chain: `AI_OPERATOR_CONSTITUTION.md` → `AI_WORK_PROTOCOL.md` → task-specific doctrine.
- Cursor reads only authorized/required files; accessibility ≠ permission.
- Plan first, approve, build new versioned file (v2, v3…), validate, human browser review, scoped commit at plateau.
- No `git add .`; verify staged set before commit; commit success via `git log`.
- Browser validation hierarchy: syntax → HTTP/server → visual → interaction → human product review.

### Data / Backend
- Pages are presentation layers; normalized city bundle is future source of truth.
- Many domain-specific APIs, not one monolith provider.
- Cache by city with domain-specific TTLs; favorites and comparison-driven preloading likely.
- Remote/non-city locations need nearest-settlement logic (future).

## 5. Rejected Paths

- Tourism drift (attractions, restaurants, nightlife, vacation itineraries).
- City rankings / “Best Cities” / prescriptive scoring.
- Astrology inside city profiles.
- Excessive CTAs (Save/Compare/Favorite everywhere).
- Oracle AI that decides for the user.
- Overwriting prototype versions instead of creating vN+1.

## 6. Deferred Work

- Typography and final color palette.
- Mobile design pass (desktop-first for now).
- Tutorial/help systems until real beta users.
- AI onboarding until profile architecture stable.

- City profile map (removed from v1; return TBD).

## 9. Governance Rules (Quick Reference)

- Constitution first; protocol second; task doctrine last.
- No coding before approved plan (full plan for new surfaces; micro-corrections after approval).
- New prototype versions, never overwrite.
- Nothing committed or pushed without explicit approval.
- Operator browser review is decisive; Cursor closeout is handoff, not acceptance.
- Anti-sprawl: useful ≠ necessary; astrology remains primary.

## 10. Unresolved Tensions

- Astrology vs city intelligence balance.
- Customization vs consistency (glyphs, colors, layouts).
- Richness vs restraint.
- Design refinement vs backend implementation start.
- Whether city intelligence could accidentally outshine astrology.

## 11. What Changed Psychologically

Chat 10’s defining outcome is not a single feature but a **transition**: the project moved from “What could this be?” toward “How do we finish this?” — more structured, more disciplined, more implementation-oriented, and more willing to say “not now.”
