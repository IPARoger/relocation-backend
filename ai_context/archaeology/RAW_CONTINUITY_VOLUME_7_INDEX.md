# RAW CONTINUITY VOLUME 7 — Companion Index

This index is an ingestion aid for future AI agents. It does **not** replace `ai_context/archaeology/RAW_CONTINUITY_VOLUME_7.md`. The full raw volume remains canonical.

## 1. Executive Abstract

Volume 7 captures the project’s transition from renderer research into product platform formation. The relocation map is no longer just a visual experiment; it is becoming a high-trust relocation astrology product with chart persistence, saved views, deep links, professional sharing, and explicit governance infrastructure.

The core philosophical arc is: truth first, beauty after truth, productization after named renderer blockers are resolved. The volume records how validation, topology, screen-space truth, adaptive refinement, Ghost Boss governance, and deferred-excellence doctrine matured enough to prevent endless renderer loops and justify the Phase 2 product pivot.

## 2. Major Project Pivots

- **From generic astrology possibility to focused relocation platform:** The project narrowed around relocation astrology, map-based discovery, chart identity, professional workflows, and contemplative UX.
- **From visual correctness to measured truth:** The team moved from “does this look right?” to brute-force walls, screen-pixel truth, topology metrics, smoke gates, and validation narratives.
- **From renderer panic to representation diagnosis:** Many apparent failures became understood as sampling, topology, overlap, or abstraction issues rather than astrology math failures.
- **From legacy-only rendering to canonical debug research:** Canonical screen-space rendering became measurable and useful, but intentionally remained debug-gated.
- **From renderer research to productization:** The readiness gate concluded the legacy renderer was MVP-ready and remaining renderer work should be blocker/trust driven.
- **From AI-assisted coding to AI-governed engineering:** Governance artifacts, archaeology, deferred excellence, smoke discipline, and closeout protocols became infrastructure.

## 3. Key Doctrines Created Or Reinforced

- **One instability source at a time.**
- **Truth before beauty, beauty after truth.**
- **Click is truth:** broad overlays guide discovery; exact clicked locations resolve factual detail.
- **Build the wall first, then intelligently back off.**
- **Continuous topology matters more than discrete overlap for thin loci.**
- **No hidden substrate switching or silent renderer migration.**
- **Renderer/aesthetic work continues only if it removes a named blocker or protects product trust.**
- **Governance artifacts are infrastructure, not meta-documentation.**
- **Deferred Excellence preserves hidden robustness, not a shiny feature wishlist.**
- **Future AI must not optimize for conversational relief over rigor.**

## 4. Renderer Conclusions

- Production renderer remains `legacy_search_regions`.
- Canonical renderer remains diagnostic/debug-only, gated, measurable, and reversible.
- `/screen-pixel-truth` plus adaptive refinement is the validated truth substrate for visible overlays.
- The proven adaptive policy is targeted, not globally slow: edge, thin-line, high-latitude, and lat-cap boundary escalation.
- Brute-force wall validation is the control specimen.
- ASC/narrow-orb issues are primarily representation/sampling/topology limits, not evidence of astrology math failure.
- Seam and cap issues were heavily investigated and should not be casually reopened.
- Aesthetic work such as aura, virga, harmonic opacity, and raindrop reveal must remain downstream of validated truth/topology.

## 5. Governance Conclusions

- The project’s main protection against AI drift is governance infrastructure, not “better memory.”
- Mandatory closeouts must include doctrine/deferred-excellence decisions, blocker classification, validation status, rollback scope, rejected scope, and next-step recommendation.
- Ghost Boss governance protects invisible robustness, institutional memory, anti-drift discipline, and non-demoable engineering intent.
- Validation narratives are part of the knowledge system because passing tests alone do not explain what changed or why.
- Archaeology and continuity volumes are infrastructure for phase transfer, not optional archival material.

## 6. Productization Conclusions

- Renderer maturity is sufficient to move into product scaffolding.
- Phase 2.x direction is chart-centric: library, saved views, handoff, deep links, onboarding, sharing, future accounts/auth/payments.
- Map handoff and saved-view replay must remain renderer-inert: no automatic `Find regions`, no hidden overlay activation, no silent renderer mutation.
- Professional sharing is a core commercialization path.
- Saved-view condition replay is the next trust gap because viewport-only links restore place but not full interpretive context.

## 12. Glossary Of Project-Specific Terms

### Ghost Boss

The project’s governance conscience: a doctrine that protects hidden robustness, institutional memory, validation discipline, anti-drift behavior, rollback thinking, and long-term trust from short-term product pressure or AI overconfidence.

### Deferred Excellence Registry

`docs/DEFERRED_EXCELLENCE_REGISTRY.md`. A registry for deferred hidden robustness and engineering intent: infrastructure, reliability, topology, cache, CI, observability, security, migration, governance, and product-trust concerns. It is not primarily a feature wishlist.

### Truth Wall

The brute-force validation reference. It classifies every relevant pixel/cell so adaptive, sparse, or optimized approaches can be compared against an exhaustive truth surface.

### Screen-Pixel Truth

The doctrine that visible overlays should be classified in screen space, based on what the user actually sees, rather than relying on coarse geographic grid centers.

### Canonical Renderer

The debug-gated screen-space/canonical truth renderer and related diagnostics. It is closer to the validated truth substrate but is not production default yet.

### Legacy Substrate

The current production renderer path, `legacy_search_regions`. It remains MVP-ready and production default while canonical work stays gated.

### Click Is Truth

The UX doctrine that broad map overlays are exploratory guidance while exact clicked locations/popups resolve detailed factual classification.

### Virga

The faint ghost/reveal visual language explored for progressive refinement: subtle hints of hidden structure that clarify as truthful overlay evidence converges.

### Topology Extraction

Diagnostic process for deriving continuous paths/structure from classified positive samples, especially important for thin loci where pixel overlap alone is misleading.

### Anti-Death-Spiral Doctrine

The rule that renderer/math/architecture work should continue only when it removes a named production blocker or protects future product trust. It prevents endless research loops after MVP readiness is established.
