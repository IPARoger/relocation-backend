Gold / Silver by Planet Gender — deliberate, meaningful color

Unlike the row-tracking studies, this color encodes real astrological information: each planet is tinted by its classical gender. The “bias” is intentional and teaches something true, rather than leaking an accidental mood. The gender scheme is governed by the switchable Layer 2 ontology.

Gold = masculine / diurnal (Sun, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Pluto)  ·  Silver = feminine / nocturnal (Moon, Venus, Neptune)  ·  neutral (Mercury, Chiron). Six application styles, faint → explicit.

1 · Row fill (faint)

gold = masculine, silver = feminine

2 · Row fill (stronger)

same, more saturated

3 · Left edge bar

color only on the planet edge

4 · Name tint

planet name colored, no fill

5 · Glyph dot

small color dot before name

6 · Fill + edge

faint fill plus edge accent
