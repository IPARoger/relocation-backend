Directional Weave Study — equal ink, opposite orientation

The key move: odd and even rows carry the same amount of ink — they differ only in the direction of the hatching. Because neither row is lighter, darker, raised or recessed, there is no “more/less” or “good/bad” signal — just a textural shimmer the eye can still follow. Shown on warm (left) and cool-neutral (right) cards to prove it is hue-independent.
