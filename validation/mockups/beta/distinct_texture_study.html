Distinct Texture Study — equal ink, different pattern

Per your mockup: odd and even rows carry the same amount of grey and differ only in pattern — direction, phase, orientation, or motif. The goal is that the user doesn't consciously see the texture, only senses that adjacent rows are different. Lines are faint (~5% ink) and need not meet or align. Toggle the row rules to judge texture alone.

1 · Direction / vs \

fine diagonals, opposite lean, equal ink

2 · Phase offset

same lines, shifted half a step

3 · Warp vs weft

vertical threads / horizontal threads

4 · Lines vs dots

diagonal hatch / dot field

5 · Dots, offset grid

same dots, staggered position

6 · Texture vs none

only even rows get the faintest hatch

7 · Left signature only

hatch on label edge (like your mockup)

8 · Near-threshold

direction, even finer + lighter

9 · Single vs sparse cross

same ink, weave looks different
