DECISION 5"Earn the line" rulethe governing principle for line vs whitespace
AWhitespace-first
Default to space. A line only when scanning across columns genuinely needs it (dense data tables). Most pages stay airy.
BLine-first
Default to a hairline everywhere; remove only where it crowds. Most consistent, most "ledger."
CHybrid by density
Dense numeric tables get separators; prose/snippet lists stay whitespace-only. Section breaks always earn a rule.
