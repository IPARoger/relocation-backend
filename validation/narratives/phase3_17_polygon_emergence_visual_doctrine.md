# Phase 3.17 Polygon Emergence Visual Doctrine

## Purpose

This doctrine is the foundation for future rain / reveal behavior in the relocation map system.

It is not decoration. It is not a loading effect. It is not atmospheric visual polish.

It defines computational truth discovery: raster uncertainty resolving into vector polygon certainty.

The emergence system must make the viewer feel that real geometry is being discovered, not illustrated.

## Core Phrase

"Pixels emerging into vector graphics."

## Emotional Tone

Mozart, not Wagner.

The tone should be restrained, elegant, slightly cold, computational, premium, and inevitable.

Avoid tragedy, war, death, collapse, guilt, or sentimental rejected-region narrative. Rejected regions are not defeated. They become virga: they ascend, fade, and return to neutrality.

## Eight-Act Structure

### Act 1: Uncertain Field

- Sparse neutral/silver particles.
- No color identity.
- No shape reveal.

### Act 2: Directional Sensing

- Subtle drift toward relevant geometry.
- Still mostly neutral.

### Act 3: Local Clustering

- Micro-clusters form.
- Still not enough to reveal the full polygon.

### Act 4: Border Hunting

- Red/yellow/blue identity begins to emerge.
- Both borders search symmetrically.

### Act 5: Peak Froth

- Widest, densest border activity.
- Both sides equally frothy.
- Center not prematurely filled.

### Act 6: Border Lock

- Crisp edge becomes inevitable.
- Froth still active around both borders.
- Final polygon should now feel plausible.

### Act 7: Virga + Rebirth

- Rejected red/blue fields ascend and fade lightly.
- Yellow interior begins center-out fill.
- Rejected fields remain visible until late.

### Act 8: Silent Vector Resolution

- Smooth clean polygon.
- No particles.
- No outline stroke.
- No process trace.

## Visual Doctrine

Organic through distribution, not brush texture.

The system must be pixel/raster/native from the beginning. Early uncertainty should already feel screen-native and computational, not hand-painted.

Do not use painterly texture, watercolor, blur, smudge, or hand-drawn particles.

The final polygon must feel like the natural result of the same system. It should not appear as a separate graphic pasted on top of a particle sequence.

## QA Failures To Avoid

- Color identity too early.
- Shape revealed by Act 3.
- No real froth/reproduction.
- One side overpowering the other.
- Interior filling too early.
- Frame 7 not structurally close enough to Frame 8.
- Final vector polygon feeling magically unrelated to previous frames.

## Open Refinement Notes

- Overlay colors are placeholders.
- Need both dark and light mode variants.
- Latitude cap may complicate organic reveal later.
- Final production renderer must use actual geometry and deterministic occupancy logic.
