# Layer Sovereignty And Forbidden Crossings

## Status

This document is CANONICAL.

It defines hard constitutional boundaries between layers.

These rules are mandatory architectural constraints.


---

# Purpose

This document defines:
- layer sovereignty,
- forbidden crossings,
- contamination risks,
- and constitutional boundaries.

The purpose is to prevent:
- symbolic collapse,
- architectural mushiness,
- interpretive contamination,
- and trust failure.


---

# Core Principle

## Every layer owns a different category of intelligence.

Layers may collaborate.

They may not collapse into each other.


---

# Constitutional Rule

## Lower layers are sovereign over higher layers.

Higher layers:
- may interpret,
- contextualize,
- or explore,

but may NEVER rewrite lower-layer truth.


---

# Layer Ownership Summary

| Layer | Owns |
|---|---|
| Layer 1 | Computational truth |
| Layer 2 | Symbolic definitions |
| Layer 3 | User-intent interpretation |
| Layer 4 | Exploratory optimization |


---

# Forbidden Crossing #1

## Layer 2 may NOT alter Layer 1 truth.

Symbolic systems may not:
- move planets,
- alter geometry,
- manipulate aspects,
- or rewrite placements.

Example violation:

- “This placement is spiritually positive, so we should soften the geometry.”

Forbidden.


---

# Forbidden Crossing #2

## Layer 3 may NOT fabricate symbolic meaning.

User interpretation layers may not:
- invent symbolic definitions,
- rewrite archetypes,
- or dynamically distort ontology systems.

Interpretation must remain grounded in Layer 2 definitions.


---

# Forbidden Crossing #3

## Layer 4 may NOT override user intentionality.

Optimization systems may:
- suggest,
- compare,
- or surface alternatives.

They may not:
- hijack sessions,
- aggressively redirect users,
- or impose symbolic agendas.


---

# Forbidden Crossing #4

## Runtime systems may NOT manipulate symbolic truth.

Renderers,
hydrators,
observers,
and runtime orchestration systems
must remain infrastructure only.

Infrastructure must not:
- invent symbolism,
- alter interpretation,
- or manipulate ontology.


---

# Forbidden Crossing #5

## AI systems may NOT fabricate certainty.

AI systems must:
- tolerate ambiguity,
- admit uncertainty,
- and remain epistemically honest.

The AI must never:
- hallucinate symbolic certainty,
- invent confidence,
- or manipulate emotionally vulnerable users.


---

# Forbidden Crossing #6

## Interpretation may NOT become morality.

The system must avoid:
- simplistic good/bad framing,
- archetypal moralism,
- and deterministic judgment language.

Astrology is symbolic and relational,
not moral absolutism.


---

# Forbidden Crossing #7

## Optimization may NOT become destiny theater.

The system must not:
- imply cosmic inevitability,
- perform mystical certainty,
- or psychologically pressure users into relocation decisions.

The platform is:
- exploratory,
- symbolic,
- and strategic.

Not prophetic authority.


---

# Forbidden Crossing #8

## AI systems may NOT bypass Layer 2 doctrine.

Layer 4 optimization and AI systems
must remain grounded in:
- Layer 2 symbolic definitions,
- and Layer 3 intentionality.

AI systems may not:
- invent private symbolic systems,
- create hidden ontologies,
- or manipulate the symbolic substrate.


---

# Important Principle

## Separation increases trust.

When layers remain sovereign:
- truth remains inspectable,
- interpretation remains flexible,
- and architecture remains extensible.

When layers collapse:
- symbolic mush emerges.


---

# Constitutional Goal

The platform should remain:
- modular,
- disciplined,
- epistemically clean,
- and professionally trustworthy.

Layer sovereignty is foundational.
