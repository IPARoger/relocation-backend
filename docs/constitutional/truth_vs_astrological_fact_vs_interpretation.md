# Truth vs Astrological Fact vs Interpretation

## Purpose

This document defines one of the most important distinctions in the platform:

- Truth
- Astrological Fact
- Interpretation

These are not the same thing.

Confusing them causes:

- symbolic mushiness,
- epistemic collapse,
- false certainty,
- and professional distrust.

---

# Layer Distinction

## Truth

Truth belongs primarily to Layer 1.

## Astrological Fact

Astrological fact belongs primarily to Layer 2.

## Interpretation

Interpretation belongs primarily to Layer 3.

---

# Truth

Truth refers to:

- astronomical reality,
- coordinate geometry,
- placements,
- aspects,
- angularity,
- house positions,
- and computational facts.

Truth is:

- deterministic,
- inspectable,
- and independently verifiable.

## Examples Of Truth

- Saturn is in the 8th house.
- Venus is within 2 degrees of the Ascendant.
- Mars squares the IC.
- Jupiter trines the Midheaven.
- This coordinate satisfies the search condition.

These are geometric truths.

They are not interpretations.

---

# Astrological Fact

Astrological fact refers to:

- symbolic definitions,
- archetypal relationships,
- dignity systems,
- orb doctrines,
- and interpretive frameworks.

These are framework-dependent symbolic definitions.

They are not pure geometry.

## Examples Of Astrological Fact

- Many Western systems associate Saturn in the 8th house with pressure around dependency, shared resources, or vulnerability.
- Venus conjunct the Ascendant is often associated with social attractiveness or relational ease.
- Tight conjunctions are often considered stronger than wide conjunctions.
- Moon archetypes are traditionally associated with emotional regulation, memory, and nurture.

These are symbolic definitions.

They are not guaranteed life outcomes.

---

# Interpretation

Interpretation applies:

- context,
- intention,
- life circumstances,
- and human meaning

onto symbolic material.

Interpretation is:

- contextual,
- collaborative,
- and probabilistic.

Interpretation is not absolute truth.

## Examples Of Interpretation

- Your fundraising difficulties may relate to Saturnian pressure around 8th-house dependency structures.
- This relocation may reduce chronic relational friction.
- This region appears emotionally stabilizing but professionally slower.
- This chart configuration may support visibility and leadership.

These are interpretive conclusions.

They are not computational facts.

---

# Important Principle

## Truth must remain sovereign.

Interpretation may:

- build upon truth,
- contextualize truth,
- or explore truth,

but may never rewrite truth.

---

# Another Important Principle

## Astrological systems may disagree.

Different symbolic systems may:

- define meanings differently,
- define orbs differently,
- define dignity differently,
- or prioritize archetypes differently.

The platform must permit disagreement.

---

# Interpretation Is Collaborative

Interpretation should:

- converge gradually,
- remain humble,
- and tolerate uncertainty.

The system must not:

- pretend omniscience,
- force symbolic certainty,
- or collapse ambiguity prematurely.

---

# Constitutional Goal

The platform intentionally separates:

- geometry,
- symbolism,
- and interpretation

so each layer remains:

- inspectable,
- modular,
- extensible,
- and trustworthy.
