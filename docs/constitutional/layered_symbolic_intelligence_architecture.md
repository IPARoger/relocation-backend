## Status

# Purpose

This document defines the core architectural philosophy of the platform.

The platform is intentionally divided into distinct symbolic intelligence layers.

These layers exist to:
- preserve truth integrity,
- prevent symbolic contamination,
- maintain modularity,
- and support long-term extensibility.


---

# Core Principle

## Lower layers may inform higher layers.

## Higher layers may NEVER rewrite lower layers.

Truth flows upward.

Interpretation never flows downward.


---

# The Four Primary Layers

The platform is divided into four major conceptual layers.

Each layer owns a different category of intelligence.


---

# Layer 1 — Truth Layer

## Purpose

Layer 1 computes:
- astronomical truth,
- relocation geometry,
- coordinate validity,
- angularity,
- aspects,
- house positions,
- and computational reality.

This layer is:
- deterministic,
- inspectable,
- objective,
- and independently verifiable.

Layer 1 owns:
# WHAT IS.


---

## Layer 1 Characteristics

Layer 1:
- does not interpret,
- does not optimize,
- does not moralize,
- and does not psychologically frame results.

It computes truth only.


---

## Examples

Examples of Layer 1 outputs:

- Saturn is in the 8th house.
- Venus is conjunct the Ascendant by 2 degrees.
- Mars squares the IC.
- This coordinate satisfies the search condition.

These are geometric truths.


---

# Layer 2 — Symbolic Definition Layer

## Purpose

Layer 2 defines:
- symbolic meanings,
- archetypal systems,
- orb doctrines,
- dignity systems,
- and interpretive frameworks.

Layer 2 owns:
# WHAT SYMBOLS MEAN.


---

## Layer 2 Characteristics

Layer 2 is:
- framework-dependent,
- modular,
- replaceable,
- and ontology-driven.

Different symbolic systems may disagree.

The platform must permit disagreement.


---

## Examples

Examples of Layer 2 definitions:

- Saturn in the 8th is associated with pressure around dependency or shared resources.
- Venus conjunct the Ascendant is associated with relational attractiveness.
- Tight conjunctions are often considered stronger than wide conjunctions.

These are symbolic definitions.

They are not objective geometry.


---

# Layer 3 — Intentional Interpretation Layer

## Purpose

Layer 3 interprets symbolic material relative to:
- user goals,
- context,
- life circumstances,
- priorities,
- and tradeoffs.

Layer 3 owns:
# WHAT MATTERS TO THE USER.


---

## Layer 3 Characteristics

Layer 3 is:
- collaborative,
- contextual,
- conversational,
- and probabilistic.

It should:
- refine,
- clarify,
- and converge.

It should not:
- dominate,
- fabricate certainty,
- or manipulate.


---

## Examples

Examples of Layer 3 interpretation:

- This relocation may reduce chronic financial pressure.
- This placement may support visibility and leadership.
- This region appears relationally supportive but professionally slower.

These are contextual interpretations.


---

# Layer 4 — Exploratory Optimization Layer

## Purpose

Layer 4 explores:
- adjacent opportunities,
- alternative symbolic strategies,
- optimization pathways,
- and unrequested but potentially valuable discoveries.

Layer 4 owns:
# WHAT ELSE MAY BE POSSIBLE.


---

## Layer 4 Characteristics

Layer 4 is:
- optional,
- exploratory,
- subordinate to user intentionality,
- and invitation-sensitive.

Layer 4 must never:
- override explicit goals,
- aggressively hijack sessions,
- or manipulate user direction.


---

## Examples

Examples of Layer 4 behavior:

- Suggesting alternative cities with stronger overall symbolic support.
- Identifying unexpected high-quality chart combinations.
- Surfacing tradeoff opportunities.
- Suggesting nearby symbolic improvements.

These are exploratory possibilities,
not deterministic mandates.


---

# Layer Relationships

The layers intentionally remain independent.

Layer 1:
- computes truth.

Layer 2:
- defines symbolic meaning.

Layer 3:
- interprets relative to user goals.

Layer 4:
- explores additional possibilities.


---

## The layers must remain sovereign.

If symbolic interpretation contaminates geometry:
- truth collapses.

If optimization overrides intentionality:
- trust collapses.

If uncertainty is hidden:
- epistemic integrity collapses.
