## Status

This document is CANONICAL.

It defines:
- purification audits,
- contamination detection,
- architectural integrity checks,
- and rollback discipline.

---

# Purpose

As the platform evolves,
systems may gradually:
- drift,
- contaminate neighboring layers,
- accumulate hidden assumptions,
- or violate constitutional doctrine.

Purification audits exist to:
- detect contamination early,
- preserve architectural integrity,
- and prevent symbolic collapse.


---

# Core Principle

## Architectural purity is easier to preserve than to restore.

Small violations accumulate.

Purification audits exist to catch them before they metastasize.


---

# What A Purification Audit Is

A purification audit is a structured review of:
- boundaries,
- responsibilities,
- sovereignty,
- contamination,
- and hidden crossings.

It asks:

- Does this system still belong to its declared layer?
- Has interpretation contaminated truth?
- Has optimization contaminated intentionality?
- Has runtime contaminated symbolism?
- Has symbolic mush emerged?
