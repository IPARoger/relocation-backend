# UX and Design Language (From Archaeology)

---

## Map-first and spatial reading

- **Map dominance:** Controls exist to serve exploration; they must not steal the primary visual field during validation or professional use.
- **Panel vs drawer tension:** Fixed panels repeatedly **hid map evidence** (lines behind UI). Future direction: adjacent panel, collapsible drawer, draggable rail—anything that preserves inspectability.
- **Global map ergonomics:** Users must pan freely near **Pacific/dateline/polar** regions during validation; artificial snap-back is disqualifying for this product class.

---

## Trust UX vs explanation UX

- **Lay users cannot be expected to reconcile** overlay edges with chart tables; that is a **developer failure mode**, not a user skill issue.
- **Professionals still need an oracle:** Right-click / precise coordinate inspection is framed as **truth instrumentation**. It must have onboarding (hint, mode toggle), and mobile needs long-press equivalent.

---

## Typography and popups (professional validation patterns)

- Popups evolved toward **lean relocated truth**: emphasize relocated angles and relocated house table; avoid cluttering with globally static natal detail in the same surface.
- **Popup placement/clipping** is trust-critical: clipped popups read as broken software, not aesthetics.
- **Clear stale state:** clearing popups on new searches prevents false confidence.

---

## Interaction pitfalls called out repeatedly

- **Dropdown correctness is infrastructure:** duplicated options, accidental advances, wrong aspect groupings—trust-breaking, not cosmetic.
- **Debug UX must allow rapid re-runs:** disabling buttons after one run blocked validation flows.
- **Hidden-Alaska-line lesson:** if controls obscure geography, users misdiagnose geometry.

---

## Emotional tone

- **Premium restraint:** contemplative, serious instrument; not toy occult UI, not “fast-food primaries.”
- **Anti-cleverness:** clever UI that hides obvious affordances is an anti-goal.
- **Long-session comfort:** astrologers may use this for hours; reduce eye strain and mental overhead.

---

## Product positioning language (from archaeology)

- **“Relocation astrology to the fore”** vs “another astrocartography clone.”
- **Exploration as pleasure** (“shopping the map”)—but only if the experience is trustworthy.

---

## Tensions to preserve (not resolve here)

- **Opacity blending elegance** vs **semantic child-colors for overlaps**—both appear; they may need a designed system, not accidental stacking.
- **Search prominence** vs **chart secondary**—DeepSeek extraction emphasizes collapsing chart real estate; other chats emphasize validation popups. Needs a coherent layout strategy across desktop/tablet/mobile.

---

## Chat 08 update: style presets and mobile layer control

Future visual style presets may be desirable, but they must remain downstream from truth geometry. Candidate families include:

- Buck Rogers / technical;
- crunchy organic;
- premium lifestyle;
- gentle new age, cautiously and optionally.

These presets may alter palette, material finish, contrast, and emotional language. They must not alter transport math, side-local normalization, popup truth, or implied astrological certainty.

Layer controls are likely necessary as overlap density increases, but mobile must avoid Photoshop-style layer-panel complexity. Preferred direction:

- compact drawer or progressive disclosure;
- quick mute/solo/focus actions;
- send-to-background and send-to-foreground for inspection;
- explicit negative / NOT / exclusion mode only when user-declared;
- quick isolation without turning the map into a graphics-editor UI.

The product tone should remain contemplative and premium. “Gentle new age” may be explored only if it avoids mystical fog, spiritual cliché, and glow theatre.
